# Lullio

An ADHD-friendly planner: one task at a time, matched to the user's energy.

## Language

### Support

**Contact message**:
A message a user sends the team from Settings → Support → Contact us, with a topic and free text.
_Avoid_: Support ticket, ticket, feedback

**Topic**:
The kind of contact message the user picks: report a bug, suggest an idea, account & subscription, or something else.
_Avoid_: Category, type

**Reply address**:
The email the team answers a contact message at: the account's real email, or one the sender types in when the account has none.
_Avoid_: Contact email
