✦ Features

Built for your brain,
not against it.

Every feature is designed to reduce friction, fight overwhelm, and keep you moving forward — even on your hardest days.

🌊

Mood-Based Scheduling

Slide to tell Lullio how you're feeling. It instantly matches tasks to your current energy level — no willpower needed to decide what to do next.

🎙️

Voice-to-Task

Just speak your thoughts out loud. Lullio transcribes and breaks them into clean, actionable tasks. No typing, no friction, no lost ideas.

🎯

One Task at a Time

No overwhelming lists. Lullio surfaces exactly one task — the right one for your current mood. Complete it, earn points, then get the next one.

⬆️

Leveling & XP

Every completed task earns XP based on difficulty. Level up with an RPG-style progression curve. Start at Level 1, climb to Legend status at Level 25+.

🔥

Streaks

Complete at least one task daily to build your streak. Watch your consecutive days grow. Premium users get a Streak Freeze to forgive missed days.

🏅

8 Achievements

Unlock badges for milestones: First Step, On a Roll (3-day streak), Unstoppable (7-day), Hard Mode, Weekly Warrior, and more. Collect them all.

📱

Home Screen Widgets

Three native widgets for your home screen: Streak counter, Today's Task, and Mood Check-in. Stay on track without even opening the app.

🤖

AI-Powered Matching

Our AI scores each task's difficulty, then intelligently matches tasks to your energy. Like having a coach who always knows what to assign next.
