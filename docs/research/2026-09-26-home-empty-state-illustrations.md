# Home empty-state illustration research

Research date: 2026-09-26. Scope: online references to inform the mascot scene before image approval. No screen design or generated image is included.

## Main finding

Choose the meaning of the empty state before choosing a pose. A screen with no items added yet needs orientation; a screen emptied by completing work needs acknowledgment. Carbon explicitly separates first-use/no-data states from confirmation after user action. Its illustration guidance says to relate the image to the situation. [Carbon empty states](https://preview.carbondesignsystem.com/building-blocks/core/patterns/empty-states)

A resting mascot alone does not distinguish these situations. That is an interpretation for Lullio, rather than a finding about a particular app.

Material provides a concrete character-plus-empty-container example; the planner/tray scene below is an original adaptation of that relationship, not an observed screenshot of a planner app.

## Direction to discuss before generation

For **nothing added/planned yet**, an awake, gently curious Lullio interacting with an unmistakably blank planner or empty task tray would connect the dog to the missing content. The object should be the visual focus alongside the dog, with no tick marks, trophy, or confetti. An empty planner is more specific to planning; a tray is a clearer empty-container metaphor but may suggest an inbox.

For **all work completed**, a completed checklist and a satisfied expression would be coherent. A resting pose only becomes meaningful when the completion context is made explicit.

These scene ideas are recommendations, not designs copied from the cited products. Preserve the supplied Pekingese identity and line-art constraints. Keep interface text outside the illustration. The user must approve the newly generated mascot image before screen design begins.
